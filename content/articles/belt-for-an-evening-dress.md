Evening dresses are often heavy, shiny or full, and sometimes the body gets lost inside them. A waist belt does several things at once:

- **It defines the waist.** Tied at the narrowest point, it gives an hourglass shape, and the dress falls better above and below.
- **It breaks the length.** On a dress in one colour from top to bottom, it divides it gently and gives the eye a place to rest.
- **It adds personality.** A big bow, lace, croc texture or ruffles: each tells a different style.
- **It renews an old dress.** The same dress with a different belt is a new look, without buying another dress.

Our belts are made of imported PU leather, soft and light, so they never feel heavy on the dress or pull the fabric. They wrap and tie, and we can make a custom size on request: just write it in the order notes.

## Choosing by fabric and colour

### Satin

Satin shines on its own, so a plain leather belt balances it well. A [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) close to the dress's colour, or in a deep shade such as the [Wide Tie Burgundy](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-burgundy/), looks refined.

### Chiffon

Chiffon is light and floaty and needs a belt that defines the waist without weighing it down. Lace is ideal here: delicate and feminine. See the [Lace](/collections/vicuna-belts/lace/) design.

### Velvet

Velvet is rich and warm and loves texture. A croc belt with its raised pattern makes a lovely contrast, especially the [Thin Tie Croc Wine](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-croc-wine/).

### Sequins and embellished dresses

A dress with sequins or heavy embroidery needs no competition. Choose a plain belt in one colour, either the same as the dress so it looks part of it, or black for a clean line.

### A note on colour

- Black dress: anything goes, but gold, silver and red feel the most like evening.
- White or off-white dress: try gold, caramel, or black for a strong contrast.
- Coloured dress: stay with black or metallics, or a deeper shade from the same family.

## The right belt for each occasion

### An engagement

Your own or someone close to you: a chance for a big bow. A Wide Tie in [white](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-white/) or burgundy on a plain dress looks festive.

### A wedding guest

A guest wants to look lovely and different without stealing the spotlight. Lace is a delicate choice here, or ruffles for movement. See the [Ruffle](/collections/vicuna-belts/ruffle/) design.

### Dinner or a night out

Dinner calls for quiet elegance. A plain midi dress with a croc belt in wine or pink looks modern and grown up. See the [Thin Tie Croc Wine](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-croc-wine/) and the [Thin Tie Croc Pink](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-croc-pink/).

## One dress, four looks

Instead of buying a dress for every occasion, keep one plain dress with a good cut and change the belt:

1. **First time:** a burgundy Wide Tie, festive for an engagement.
2. **Second time:** the [Lace White](/collections/vicuna-belts/lace-white/), delicate for a wedding.
3. **Third time:** croc wine, modern for dinner.
4. **Fourth time:** the [Ruffle Red](/collections/vicuna-belts/ruffle-red/), playful and feminine.

### Small tips when tying

- Tie it at the narrowest point of your waist, not low, so your legs look longer.
- Keep the bow in the middle, or slide it slightly to one side for a different look.
- Do not pull it too tight on light fabrics such as chiffon, so the fabric does not crease.
- If the dress is full below, a wide belt balances the volume better than a slim one.

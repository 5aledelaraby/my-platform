If you follow the runways and fashion magazines this year, you will have noticed that **the belt is a star again**. Not just something that holds trousers up, but a key piece that changes the whole outfit. The good news: the 2026 trends are easy to wear, for work, evenings out and occasions alike.

## 1. The statement wide belt

The biggest trend of the year: a wide belt wrapped around the whole waist, the centrepiece of the look. Wear it over a plain dress, over a blazer, or over a loose shirt. The secret is to keep the rest of the outfit quiet and let the belt speak.

Our [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) is made for exactly this: 14 cm wide, tied in a big bow at the front or a knot with long ends.

## 2. The bow at the waist

Bows are everywhere this year, on hair, bags and shoes, and of course at the waist. A big bow at the front feels feminine and playful; tied to one side, it looks more polished and calm.

## 3. Earthy tones and burgundy

Black and brown are always here, but this year warm earthy shades such as cognac, camel and beige are strong, together with **burgundy** and soft green. Try the [Wide Tie Burgundy](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-burgundy/) on black or beige, or the [Wide Tie Green](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-green/) on white.

## 4. Metallic shine

A touch of shine is back, especially for evenings: a gold or silver belt on a plain dress makes a complete evening look with no other jewellery.

## 5. Texture: croc

A raised pattern gives a plain outfit personality with no effort. The [Thin Tie Croc Wine](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-croc-wine/) is rich and goes with black and beige; the [Thin Tie Croc Pink](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-croc-pink/) is bolder and younger.

## 6. Ruffles and soft details

For lovers of softness, a ruffle at the waist adds movement to any dress. The [Ruffle Red](/collections/vicuna-belts/ruffle-red/) is lovely on a white or beige dress.

## Which trend is yours?

- **If you like calm:** an earthy shade or black, with a small bow or a simple knot.
- **If you like to stand out:** a wide belt in a bold colour or with shine.

The best thing about a wrap belt is that it is **one belt with many looks**. Tie it in a bow today, a knot with long ends tomorrow and a double wrap after that, and it looks like a different belt each time. Here are five ways, each in a few steps.

Before we start: the [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) is 140 cm long and 14 cm wide. The wide part sits at the front, and the two straps go around the back and come back to the front. That is the base of every way below.

## 1. The classic bow in the middle

1. Place the wide part on the front of your waist.
2. Take both straps around the back, cross them and bring them to the front.
3. Tie a simple bow in the middle, like a shoelace.
4. Make both loops the same size and let the ends hang.

Perfect for plain dresses and outings, and lovely with the [Wide Tie White](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-white/).

## 2. The side bow

Exactly the same steps, but **move the knot to one side** before tying, a little above the hip bone. A side bow looks more grown up and polished, and it is great for work.

## 3. A single knot with long ends

One of the easiest and most elegant:

1. Wrap the belt as before.
2. Tie a single knot, not a bow.
3. Let both ends fall over the dress, or keep one longer than the other.

The falling ends draw a vertical line that lengthens the body. Try it with the [Wide Tie Burgundy](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-burgundy/) or the [Wide Tie Green](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-green/).

## 4. The double wrap

If your waist is small or you want the belt to look slimmer:

1. Wrap the straps around your waist **twice** instead of once.
2. Tie a small bow or a knot.

The belt sits closer and neater and the bow comes out smaller and finer. It also suits the [Thin Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie/).

## 5. The hidden knot

For a clean look with no visible bow:

1. Wrap the belt and tie the knot at the back instead of the front.
2. Tuck both ends under the belt at the sides.

The belt looks like one piece wrapped around the waist: calm and modern.

## Tips to keep the belt in place

- **Tie it at the narrowest point of your waist**, where it will not slip.
- **Do not pull it too tight:** the Wide Tie has soft Lycra that shapes itself to the waist.
- **Check both ends in the mirror** before you go out, so they are even, or uneven on purpose.
- **With heavy fabrics** such as abayas and coats, a big bow or a single knot works better than a double wrap.

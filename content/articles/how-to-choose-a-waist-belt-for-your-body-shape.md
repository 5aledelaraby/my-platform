Every body is beautiful in its own way, and a waist belt is not there to hide anything. It is there to show the part of you that you love and to make an outfit look finished and intentional. The question we hear most is simple: which belt suits my shape? The answer comes down to three things: where you wear it, how wide it is, and how big the bow or knot is.

![A royal blue thin tie belt tied in a bow over a white dress](/img/looks/thin-tie-royal-blue-1200.webp)

## Three rules that work for every body

### Where to wear it

The best place for any belt is your natural waist: the narrowest point of your middle, usually a little above the navel. A belt there divides the body in balanced proportions. If you want your legs to look longer, raise it a centimetre or two, which works especially well with full dresses and high-waisted skirts.

### Wide or slim?

A wide belt defines the waist clearly and becomes the star of the outfit; a belt that ties with a slim strap gives a softer, quieter shape. ### The size of the bow

A big bow draws the eye to the waist and adds volume; a small bow or a single knot keeps things soft. Long ends falling down the front draw a vertical line that makes you look taller.

## A guide for each shape

These are ideas, not rules. If you love something and feel good in it, it is right. Most of us sit between two shapes, so take the idea that feels like you and try it.

### Pear

Shoulders narrower than hips and a defined waist: your waist deserves to show. Wear the belt at the natural waist or slightly above, with a big bow that lifts the eye. The [Wide Tie White](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-white/) looks lovely on a dress that flares below.

### Apple

If your shoulders and bust stand out and your legs are slim, play with long lines. Wear the belt just under the bust over a dress or blouse that falls freely, and let long ends hang down. The [Wide Tie Burgundy](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-burgundy/) tied with long ends is a good choice, also over an open jacket or cardigan.

### Hourglass

Shoulders and hips in balance with a defined waist: almost any belt works. Wear it exactly at the natural waist and try details such as lace or ruffles. The [Lace White](/collections/vicuna-belts/lace-white/) on a plain dress is elegant; the [Ruffle Red](/collections/vicuna-belts/ruffle-red/) is playful.

![A red thin tie belt defining the waist over a light shirt and dark grey trousers](/img/looks/thin-tie-red-1200.webp)

### Rectangle

When shoulders, waist and hips are close in width, a belt is the easiest way to create curves. Choose a wide belt with volume, a big bow or ruffles, at the natural waist over a loose dress or blouse, and let the fabric gather above and below it. A belt over a long shirt or an open blazer does the same with no effort.

### Petite and tall

If you are petite, wear the belt slightly above the natural waist and choose a colour close to your outfit so the line stays unbroken; a small bow keeps the proportions right. The [Thin Tie Camel](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-camel/) goes with almost everything.

If you are tall, you have room to play: big bows, long ends and contrasting colours all look good. Try the [Wide Tie Green](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-green/) for a bold colour.

## Quick tips

- **Colour:** a belt close to your outfit's colour looks calm and long; a contrasting one draws the eye to the waist.
- **With skirts and high-waisted trousers:** tuck the top in and wrap the belt right on the waistband, so it continues the line of the skirt or trousers.
- **Bow size:** with prints or busy details, keep the bow small; with a plain outfit, let a big bow do the work.
- **Try at home:** in front of a mirror, try the belt at the natural waist, then slightly higher, and keep the one you feel best in.

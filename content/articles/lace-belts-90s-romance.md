If it feels like fashion is looking back with nostalgia lately, you are not alone. The romantic style we link with the 90s, slip dresses, soft fabrics and delicate details, is back in a new way. And one of its quietest stars is the lace belt.

## Why lace is back

The 90s mixed simple cuts with one soft, feminine detail. Today we want the same balance: comfortable clothes, but with a touch that makes them feel special. A lace belt brings two things together: the romance of lace and the hold of leather that defines the waist.

## How to wear a lace belt today

The lovely thing about a lace belt is that it needs no special outfit. It wraps and ties with a slim strap, so it adjusts to you. You can also ask for a custom size in the order notes.

### With a slip dress

The combination closest to the spirit of the 90s. A slip dress on its own can look loose or shapeless; the belt draws the waist and adds a soft frame.

### With a shirt dress

A shirt dress is practical and comfortable, but sometimes it looks too formal. A lace belt breaks that seriousness with a feminine touch. White lace on a light blue or beige shirt dress is fresh and easy.

### With an abaya or a long dress

If you love long, loose clothes, a belt is your best friend. Wrap it over an abaya to define the waist without making it tight. More ideas in [six ways to belt an abaya](/journal/style-guides/how-to-wear-a-belt-with-an-abaya/).

### Over a plain T-shirt dress

The easiest everyday look. A plain cotton T-shirt dress is comfortable but can look too simple. Add a lace belt and the look changes.

## How to care for lace

Lace is delicate, and a little care keeps the belt looking new for a long time:

- **Never machine wash or soak it.** If it gets a mark, wipe it gently with a soft cloth dampened with water only and let it air dry.
- **Keep it away from sun and heat.** Do not leave it in direct sun for long, and never iron it.
- **Store it flat or loosely rolled.** Do not fold it hard, so the lace does not crease; keep it in a fabric bag or a box.
- **Watch out for things that snag.** Chains, brooches and metal on bags can pull the threads.
- **Perfume first.** Spray your perfume a little before you put the belt on, not on it.

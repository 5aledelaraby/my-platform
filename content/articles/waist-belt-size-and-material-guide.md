The first thing to know is that all our belts tie: no buckles and no holes. That makes sizing much easier than you might think.

## Sizes: which one fits you?

### The sizes

The [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) is 14 cm wide and 140 cm long, the [Thin Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie/) is 10 cm wide and 90 cm around the waist, and the [Ruffle](/collections/vicuna-belts/ruffle/) has elastic inside for an exact fit at the waist. Because the belts tie rather than close on a set hole, they fit most bodies: with a small waist you keep longer ends for the bow, with a larger one you tie with shorter ends.

### Want a custom size?

If the usual size does not suit you, or you want a longer belt for a bigger bow, we make custom sizes on request:

- Take a soft tape measure.
- Wrap it around your waist exactly where you plan to wear the belt, a little above the navel or at your natural waist.
- Keep the tape snug but not tight, and breathe normally.
- Write the number in centimetres, and tell us if you want longer ends.
- When you place your order on the site, write the size in the order notes; we will contact you if we need anything.

## Materials

All our belts are made of imported PU leather, light and soft on the body, in different finishes, each with its own character.

### Plain PU leather

The [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) has soft Lycra that lets it shape itself to the waist and fit comfortably up to 90 kg, with a smooth, gently glossy surface. The [Thin Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie/) is plain, soft PU leather in the same spirit, like the [Thin Tie Camel](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-camel/). Clear, rich colours that are easy to clean: right for every day, work and outings.

## Care and storage

A belt that is cared for lasts for years:

- **Smooth leather:** wipe with a soft, slightly damp cloth, then dry. Avoid strong cleaners and alcohol.
- **Lace:** never in the washing machine. For a small mark, dab gently with a damp cloth without rubbing, so the threads do not pull.
- **Croc:** wipe with a soft cloth in the direction of the pattern, so dust does not settle in the grooves.

To store, roll the belt loosely or hang it flat rather than folding it, and keep it in a fabric bag or a drawer away from damp and sun. Do not put heavy things on it, especially lace and the Wide Tie.

An abaya is one of the most comfortable things you can wear, but sometimes it feels like it swallows you and your shape disappears inside it. That is where **a waist belt** comes in: one wrap at the waist defines the shape and makes the same abaya look like a different design, without shortening or tightening anything. Here are six tried ways, and how to pick the right belt for each.

## Why a belt makes a difference

An abaya is cut wide and straight, which is the secret of its comfort. But a straight cut hides the waist completely, so the body reads as one block. A belt creates a point in the middle that catches the eye, so you look taller and better proportioned. And it does not make the abaya tight: it gathers it only at the waist, and the fabric keeps flowing below.

## 1. An open abaya over a plain dress

The easiest way. Wear the abaya open over a dress, or a blouse and skirt in one colour, and tie the belt **on the dress underneath**, not on the abaya. The abaya falls from the sides like a cape and the belt shows in the middle. Try the [Wide Tie Burgundy](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-burgundy/) with its end left hanging.

## 2. A closed abaya with the belt over it

If the abaya is closed and the fabric is not too heavy, tie the belt right over it at the narrowest point of your waist. Choose a **wide** belt that holds the fabric and shows, rather than a slim one that sinks into it.

## 3. An embroidered abaya with lace

An abaya with gold or silver embroidery needs a belt that completes it, not one that competes with it. Lace goes with embroidery as if it were part of it, especially for evenings and weddings. Try the [Lace White](/collections/vicuna-belts/lace-white/) on a black or navy abaya.

## 4. Kimonos and long cardigans

The same idea as the open abaya. A long cardigan or kimono looks far smarter gathered at the waist. Tie the belt over it with a knot to one side and let both ends hang. Warm shades such as cognac and camel are lovely with knitwear in winter.

## 5. The everyday abaya

On an ordinary day you do not need a loud look. A belt close to the abaya's colour, beige on beige or brown on camel, looks neat and calm. The [Thin Tie Camel](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-camel/) is an easy, comfortable choice.

## 6. A pattern for personality

On a plain abaya, a belt with a raised pattern does all the work. Our [Signature Pattern](/collections/vicuna-belts/pattern/) belt, in brown or black, turns a simple abaya into a look with character.

## How to choose the right belt for your abaya

- **Width:** heavy or loose fabric needs a wide belt; a light one can take a slimmer belt.
- **Colour:** close to the abaya for calm, contrasting for a statement.
- **Size:** the [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) is 140 cm long, wraps and ties, and fits comfortably up to 90 kg, even over an abaya.

## Questions we hear often

### Will a belt spoil the shape of a loose abaya?

No. It gathers the fabric only at the waist, and the rest keeps falling freely. If you prefer the abaya loose, wear it open and tie the belt on the dress underneath.

### Can I belt an abaya with wide sleeves?

Yes, and it is one of the best looks: wide sleeves with a defined waist balance each other.

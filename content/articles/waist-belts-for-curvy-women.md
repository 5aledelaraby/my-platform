There is a common idea that belts are "not for every body", and that a fuller figure should stay away from them. The opposite is true. The right belt, in the right place, gives a curvy body a beautifully balanced shape, because it draws the waist and divides the body in a way that is easy on the eye. It all comes down to **width, placement and material**.

## Width: wide or slim?

For a curvy figure, **a wide belt is usually your friend**. A very slim belt can disappear or cut the body with a hard line. A wide one holds the whole waist softly and creates a natural curve. Our [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) is 14 cm wide: enough to hold and define the waist without pressing on it.

## Placement: where exactly?

- **At the narrowest point of your waist.** This is the golden rule, usually a little above the navel.
- **Just under the bust.** If your waist is less defined, tie the belt higher. This empire line lengthens the body and lets a dress fall freely.
- **Away from the hips.** A belt worn low draws the eye where you may not want it.

What most of us dislike about belts is when they are stiff, press and leave marks. ## Colours that work

- **Close to your dress:** a long, unbroken line for a calm look.
- **Dark on a light dress:** defines the waist clearly. Try the [Wide Tie Burgundy](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-burgundy/) or the [Thin Tie Royal Blue](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-royal-blue/).
- **A bold colour:** absolutely allowed. The [Wide Tie Mustard](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-mustard/) on a plain outfit looks confident.

## Four looks to try

1. **A loose plain dress with a wide belt** at the narrowest point: the easiest, most elegant look.
2. **A long open cardigan with the belt over the blouse underneath:** lengthens the body and defines the waist.
3. **A leather jacket or blazer with the belt over it:** a modern touch for going out.
4. **An abaya or kimono with a [camel](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-camel/) bow and long ends:** the falling ends draw a long vertical line.

A belt is for every body. Choose **wide**, tie it at the **narrowest point** or just under the bust, and pick a **soft, comfortable** material. Still unsure?

Sometimes you have a lovely dress but it feels like something is missing, and that something is usually a belt. Here are five ways to style a belt with a dress, each giving the same dress a completely different look.

## For daytime and going out

During the day you want something comfortable and simple that still looks put together. These two ways suit work, university and casual outings.

### 1. A small bow in the middle

The classic that never goes wrong. Wrap the belt around the narrowest point of your waist and tie the slim strap in a small bow right in the middle. A small bow defines the waist without shouting, so it works with printed and plain dresses alike.

The design made for this is the [Thin Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie/): 10 cm wide and 90 cm around the waist, an everyday classic. On a light or plain dress, try a deep shade such as the [Thin Tie Royal Blue](/collections/vicuna-belts/thin-tie-royal-blue/).

### 2. A bow to one side

The same belt, with the bow slid a little to one side. This small change feels more relaxed and youthful and breaks the symmetry nicely. It works especially well with loose, casual dresses.

A small tip: put the bow on the same side as a detail of the dress, such as a pocket or a ruffle, so it looks intentional.

## For evenings and occasions

In the evening you can go bigger and let the belt be the star.

### 3. A single knot with a long end

One of the most elegant and easiest ways. Wrap a wide belt around your waist, tie a single knot, and let the long end fall over the dress. It draws a vertical line that lengthens the body and moves with you as you walk.

![A wide green waist belt tied in a single knot with a long end over a white dress](/img/looks/wide-tie-green-1200.webp)

The [Wide Tie](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie/) ties this way and comes in several colours. Green is striking on black, white and beige; mustard is a warm shade that goes with earth tones and denim. See the [Wide Tie Mustard](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-mustard/).

### 4. A double wrap and a big bow

For a very defined waist and a rich look, wrap the belt twice around your waist before tying it. The double wrap adds layers and depth and makes the belt look wider. Then tie a big bow at the front and shape it with your hands so it stands full and even.

![A wide mustard waist belt tied in a big bow at the front](/img/looks/wide-tie-mustard-1200.webp)

Calm colours such as the [Wide Tie White](/collections/vicuna-belts/wide-tie-white/) or burgundy look beautiful on plain evening dresses.

## How to choose the right colour

- **Plain dress:** the belt can stand out. Choose a contrast, such as red on black or gold on navy.
- **Printed dress:** pick a colour from the print itself, so the look feels coordinated.
- **With your bag and shoes:** they need not match, but keep them in the same family, warm with warm or cool with cool.
- **If in doubt:** black, camel and beige are safe with most dresses.
- **Texture matters too:** soft plain leather suits wool and knitwear; croc adds polish to plain fabric.

## 5. Over a blazer or a long cardigan

This turns an ordinary outfit into a very put-together one, and it is lovely in autumn and winter. Wear the dress, then an open blazer or long cardigan over it, and tie the belt over both at the narrowest point of your waist. You get the warmth of layers without looking bulky, because the belt brings the waist back.

- Choose a wide belt: it holds the layers well and looks in proportion with them.
- A single knot with a long end is lovely over a long cardigan; a small bow suits a blazer.
- Match the belt to the blazer for a calm work look, or contrast it for a bold touch.
